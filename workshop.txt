description=[h1]Snyder's Extended Bandits[/h1]
Two companion patches for the Bandits mod, bundled as one item.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Bandits Force Spawn Patch[/h1]
[list]
[*] Fixes scheduled clan spawns failing to find a valid placement point
[*] Adds two consumable Distress Beacon items under their own "Distress Beacons" inventory category:
[list]
[*] [b]Hostile Distress Beacon[/b] - calls in an armed squad from any clan with the "Assault" Spawn AI option enabled
[*] [b]Friendly Distress Beacon[/b] - calls in a squad from any clan with the "Companions" Spawn AI option enabled
[/list]
[*] Both beacons are craftable at Electrical level 3, at a deliberately steep material cost so they can't be spammed
[*] A moodle shows which clan just answered your call
[/list]

[b]Sandbox options[/b] (own page: "Bandits Force Spawn Patch"):
[list]
[*] Require Admin to call specific clans (default ON) - non-admins get a random matching clan; admins can always pick
[*] Distress Beacons Craftable (default ON) - toggles both craft recipes on/off
[*] Debug Mode (default OFF) - detailed spawn-attempt logging for troubleshooting
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Custom Dialogue Creator[/h1]
[list]
[*] Adds a "Custom Dialogue" screen to the Bandit Clan editor
[*] Write clan-specific lines tied to triggers (spotted, taking damage, breach, reload, death, burning, random ambient chatter, and more)
[*] Custom lines replace the generic dialogue for that clan when triggered
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[b]Requires:[/b] Bandits (Bandits2), Bandits Creator, and MoodleFramework
[b]Mod IDs:[/b] BanditsForceSpawnPatch, CustomDialogueCreator
